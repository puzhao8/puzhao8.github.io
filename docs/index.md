# Puzhao Zhang

**DHI**

Puzhao Zhang works at DHI, where he focuses on large-scale thematic mapping, carbon and water dynamic analysis, and environmental change monitoring. Before joining DHI, he worked as a Research Scientist at KTH, where he led several research projects. He was the Principal Investigator of the AI4Wildfire research project, funded by Swedish Digital Futures, where he developed AI-based solutions for wildfire progression monitoring. He also led task 4.7 in the EU Harmonia Project, which focused on developing innovative AI techniques for urban and surrounding environments, and participated in the ESA SAR4Wildfire Project, further extending AI in the field of SAR for environmental monitoring. In addition, he contributed to the development of the Google Earth Engine (GEE)-based SDG indicator 11.3.1 web application, designed to track the ratio of land consumption to population growth, a project that won the GEO4Good Impact Award in 2023. At DHI, he supports the reporting of SDG indicator 6.6.1, which tracks changes in water-related ecosystems. He also develops satellite-based data products for UNEP's Nature-based Solution (NbS), and is actively involved in the [Global Wetland Watch](https://www.globalwetlandwatch.org/) project, funded by Google.org. His vision is to leverage satellite big data and advanced Machine Learning/AI techniques to tackle environmental challenges and mitigate climate change, supporting a more sustainable future.

## Research interests
- Geospatial Artificial Intelligence (GeoAI)
- Remote Sensing and Machine learning for land-cover mapping
- Large-scale wetland mapping from optical and radar time series
- Change detection and time-series analysis of inundation dynamics
