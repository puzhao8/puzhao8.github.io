# Publications

## Selected publications

1. **Puzhao Zhang**, Xikun Hu, Yifang Ban, Andrea Nascetti, and Maoguo Gong (2024). Assessing Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1, and ALOS-2 PALSAR-2 Data for Large-Scale Wildfire-Burned Area Mapping: Insights from the 2017–2019 Canada Wildfires. *Remote Sensing*, 16(3), 556.

2. **Puzhao Zhang**, Yifang Ban and Andrea Nascetti (2023). Sentinel-1 SAR Time Series for Wildfire Burned Area Mapping with Total-Variation Regularized U-Net. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 203, 301-313.

3. Xikun Hu, **Puzhao Zhang**, Yifang Ban, and Maryam Rahnemoonfar (2023). GAN-based SAR and optical image translation for wildfire impact assessment using multi-source remote sensing data. *Remote Sensing of Environment*, 289, 113522.

4. Xikun Hu, **Puzhao Zhang**, and Yifang Ban (2022). Large-Scale Wildfire Burn Severity Mapping in Multispectral Imagery Using Deep Semantic Segmentation Models. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 196, 228-240.

5. **Puzhao Zhang**, Yifang Ban and Andrea Nascetti (2021). Learning U-Net without Forgetting for Near Real-Time Wildfire Monitoring by the Fusion of SAR and Optical Time Series. *Remote Sensing of Environment*, 261, 112467.

6. Yifang Ban\*, **Puzhao Zhang**\*, Andrea Nascetti et al. (2020). Near Real-Time Wildfire Progression Monitoring with Sentinel-1 SAR Time Series and Deep Learning. *Scientific Reports*, 10, 1326:1-15. (\*Equal contribution, corresponding author. 7. **Puzhao Zhang**, Andrea Nascetti, Yifang Ban, Maoguo Gong (2019). An Implicit Radar Convolutional Burn Index for Burnt Area Mapping with Sentinel-1 C-band SAR Data. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 158, 50-62.

8. **Puzhao Zhang**, Maoguo Gong, Hui Zhang, Jia Liu, Yifang Ban (2018). Unsupervised Difference Representation Learning for Detecting Multiple Types of Changes in Multitemporal Remote Sensing Images. *IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing*, 57, 2277-2289.

9. **Puzhao Zhang**, Maoguo Gong, Hui Zhang, Jia Liu (2017). DRLnet: Deep Difference Representation Learning Network and An Unsupervised Optimization Framework. In *Proceedings of the International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence (IJCAI)*, 3413-3419.

10. **Puzhao Zhang**, Maoguo Gong, Linzhi Su, Jia Liu, Zhizhou Li (2016). Change Detection Based on Deep Feature Representation and Mapping Transformation for Multi-spatial-resolution Remote Sensing Images. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 116, 24-41.
