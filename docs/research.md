# Research

## Global wetland mapping
Annual and 3-year rolling wetland products at 10 m from Sentinel-1/2, with basin-level
statistics and sampling-based validation (Global Wetland Watch).

## Surface water and inundation
Monthly and annual surface-water dynamics, SDG 6.6.1 freshwater ecosystem indicators, and
flood-extent mapping.

## Methods
- Time-series analysis and change detection (e.g. CCDC)
- Deep-learning segmentation of satellite imagery
- Stratified sampling and accuracy assessment
- Scalable processing with Google Earth Engine
