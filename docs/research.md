# Research

## Global wetland mapping

Wetland extent is mapped globally at 10 m resolution from Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2, as
annual and three-year rolling products. Maps are summarised by HydroBASINS catchment
(levels 6, 8 and 12) and assessed with a stratified, sampling-based validation design.

## Surface water and inundation

Monthly and annual surface-water dynamics are derived from satellite time series and used to
report freshwater ecosystem extent and change, including for SDG indicator 6.6.1. Related work
maps flood and inundation extent for regional case studies.

## Methods

- Time-series analysis and change detection (e.g. continuous change detection and classification)
- Deep-learning semantic segmentation of multispectral and radar imagery
- Stratified sampling, reference-data collection and area-adjusted accuracy assessment
- Scalable geospatial processing with Google Earth Engine
